thermo-nuclear (rerun on the corrected tree) WARN · C0 H0 M5 L8

Round 2 H1 (a key that a concurrent settlement inserts is written but neither locked nor re-rated, and the withdraw step could deadlock with a proposal): CLOSED. The transaction-scoped advisory lock serialises the settlements of one VA Submission. Under READ COMMITTED, the lock statement that runs after the advisory lock sees every Finding that earlier settlements inserted. Only settlements insert Findings, so every Finding a settlement writes is either locked in id order or inserted by that settlement. Proposals, BulkProposals and Findings.propose never take the advisory lock.

Deadlock analysis: no wait-for cycle in any of these pairs: settlement and settlement, two passes settling one file, settlement and proposal or Bulk-set, and settlement and register apply. The advisory lock is taken after the settlement's own report_file row locks and before its Finding locks. No transaction that holds Finding locks ever waits for it.
