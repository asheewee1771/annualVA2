  • Per-Finding audit written one row at a time (thermo-nuclear #3, code-review M1, im8 F2, springboot M1): up to 1001 single-row audit inserts, synchronisations and log lines while up to 1000 Finding rows are locked; the loop now sits in Proposals.write (Proposals.java:110-116).
