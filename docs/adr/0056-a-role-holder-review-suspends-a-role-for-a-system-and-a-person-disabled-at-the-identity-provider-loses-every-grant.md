# A role-holder review suspends a role for a System, and a person disabled at the identity provider loses every grant

Status: accepted (items 3, 6, 7 and 8 decided 2026-10-25; item 4, the exception in item 6 and the disposal of reviews decided 2026-10-26, docs/agents/reviewer-decisions.md)

Ticket 47 (story 80a's removal half, IM8 ac-3 and ac-4) builds the VA POCs' role-holder review and the immediate removal of anyone disabled at the identity provider. It adds one migration (V40), four endpoints under `/api/role-holders`, one boundary port (`IdentityStatus`) with an unwired production adapter, a setting and the Role-holder review screen. The writes to `role_grant` stay in `RoleLapse` (ADR 0055), which becomes the writer of a grant's suspension as well as its lapse and return; `RoleHolders` in `access` reads every grant for the review and changes them only through it. The review itself lives in `register` (`RoleHolderReviews`), because it compares the holders with the Register, and `access` reads neither the Register nor the calendar.

1. **What is stored.** `role_grant` gains `suspended_by` (the VA POC; null for the identity provider) and `suspension_cause` (`REVIEW` or `IDENTITY_PROVIDER`), set with V4's `suspended_at` and cleared together by a restore; a CHECK holds that the cause is set exactly when the grant is suspended. A new table `role_holder_review` keeps each completed review (when, by whom, how many holders it listed); the audit log records it too. Its only foreign key is to `app_user`, which the disposal (ADR 0050) does not delete, so the disposal order is unchanged; `suspended_by` likewise.

2. **The review** (`GET /api/role-holders`, VA POC). Every grant not revoked, as its row reads (`id`, `role`, `email`, `systemId`, `suspensionCause`, `returnedAt`, `returnTrigger` and the rest of `RoleGrant`), with the person's name, the System's ID and name, the source, the state (`ACTIVE`, `LAPSED`, `SUSPENDED` before `LAPSED`, or `EXPIRED`), the last use, lapse, suspension and automatic return, whether the Register names the person for that role on that System now (`namedInRegister`, null for a VA POC's or Auditor's role), whether they have signed in, whether the identity provider reports them disabled, whether it is the caller's own, and whether the caller may suspend or restore it (`suspendable`, `restorable`: `RoleHolder.suspendableBy` and `restorableBy`, the same rules the commands refuse by, so the screen re-derives none). The VA POCs come first, then by System ID, role and email. `automaticReturns` lists the holders whose role came back by itself (`returned_at` after the last review, ADR 0055), newest first. Reading it is not audited, as the Register list is not (reviewer decisions, 2026-10-06): it names people and Systems, no report content.

3. **When a review is due** (decided 2026-10-25). `reviewDue` with `dueBecause`: `never-reviewed`; `three-months`, once the business date (Asia/Singapore) reaches the last review's date plus 3 calendar months; and `register-applied`, when a Register apply came after the last review (ac-4: "at each Register upload"). The ticket names only the 3 months; the alternative is to drop the Register reason and rely on the VA POC applying it to review. Nothing is mailed when a review falls due: the screen's flag is the reminder.

4. **Recording a review** (`POST /api/role-holders/reviews`, VA POC, `201`; decided 2026-10-26, docs/agents/reviewer-decisions.md). It records the time, the VA POC, and the list of holders it checked: the body's `grantIds`, the grants the screen listed (each once; one the review does not list, unknown or revoked, is `400 invalid-field`), or every grant listed now when the body names none. `holders` is their number, `grant_ids` (V43) holds them, and `GET /api/role-holders` answers both on `lastReview`. It is audited `ROLE_HOLDERS_REVIEWED` with the `count` and needs no step-up (it changes no role). The alternative, kept until 2026-10-26, recorded only the count.

5. **Suspension** (`POST /api/role-holders/{grantId}/suspend`, VA POC). A step-up action (ac-2, "changing roles"; intent `role-suspend`), asked before anything is read. Only an SMO/Tech Lead or Cyber Manager grant, a role held for a System, can be suspended (`409 not-for-a-system` otherwise; the glossary's review "suspends a person's role for a System"); a suspended one is `409 already-suspended`; an unknown or revoked grant is `404`. The suspended grant stops counting on the person's next request (ADR 0011), for that System only. Audited `ROLE_SUSPENDED` with reason `review`, by the VA POC.

6. **Until a Register upload names someone else** (decided 2026-10-25). The Register is one standing list (ADR 0018), so every apply names every active System's people again; if naming the same person again lifted the suspension, any later upload by either VA POC would undo it silently. So an apply that still names the suspended person keeps the grant suspended (ADR 0055 item 4 already left a suspended grant alone), and one that names someone else revokes it and grants the new person, as it always does; since 2026-10-26 (docs/agents/reviewer-decisions.md) such a replacement is applied even on a System with in-flight work, as the one exception to ADR 0049's hold-back, so a suspension never leaves a System with nobody able to act. A VA POC lifts a suspension explicitly with **restore** (`POST /api/role-holders/{grantId}/restore`, step-up intent `role-restore`, `409 not-suspended`, audited `ROLE_RESTORED`), which the ticket's "suspend or restore their own grant" implies. The alternative reading, that any upload naming the person lifts it, needs no restore.

7. **Not one's own** (ticket). A VA POC can neither suspend nor restore a grant of their own: `403 own-grant`, audited `REFUSED` in a transaction of its own. The other VA POC can. A VA POC's own VA POC grant is not suspendable anyway (item 5); whether a VA POC may suspend the other VA POC's per-System grant is allowed as built (decided 2026-10-25: the alternative is to refuse every grant of the other VA POC too).

8. **Disabled at the identity provider** (ac-3: "loses access immediately"). Two channels see it:
   - the sign-in or step-up callback: the identity provider adapter maps a status beside `afcas.uuid` (`"afcas": {"status": ...}` or the flat `afcas.status`) into `Identity.disabled`; absent means enabled, any value but `active` means disabled. The claim name was decided on 2026-10-25 and holds until the H4 onboarding confirms what MCC SSO sends. The sign-in is linked as usual (so grants made by email attach), then refused with reason `account-disabled`;
   - the session check: a signed-in session asks `IdentityStatus.disabled(ssoId)` at most every `annualva.sign-in.identity-check-interval` (default 5 minutes; the sign-in is the first check), as one of the limits `SessionLimitsFilter` enforces beside the idle and absolute limits (it ends the session with the reason `identity-provider-disabled`). When the provider cannot answer, the session is left as it was and asked again after the interval, with a warning in the log (decided 2026-10-25: failing closed would sign everyone out whenever the provider is down). The production adapter is unwired until ticket 51 builds MCC SSO's service client, so until then only the callback channel works; the Seam 1 harness fakes the port.

   Either way `IdentityDisablement` marks `app_user.idp_disabled_at` (V2), suspends every grant of the person, VA POC grants included (`ROLE_SUSPENDED`, reason `identity-provider`, by nobody), in one transaction, then deletes every session of theirs from the store; the request that saw it is answered `401`. A later sign-in the provider allows clears `idp_disabled_at` but restores nothing: a VA POC restores each grant, and a restore is refused (`409 disabled-at-identity-provider`) while the person is still marked disabled. A VA POC disabled there is restored by the other VA POC.

Migration numbering: V40 sorts after V39, the latest merged, which also changed `role_grant`. No notification or audit-detail CHECK changes: the entries use the existing `role`, `source`, `grant_id`, `reason` and `count` keys, and no `NoticeAction` is added.

Not done here, on purpose:
- Telling the suspended person, or mailing the VA POCs when a review falls due: the spec names no such message.
- Auditor grants (Release 2): the review lists them, but only per-System roles are suspendable.
- ~~Disposal of `role_holder_review` rows~~: decided 2026-10-26 (docs/agents/reviewer-decisions.md), they are kept for the VA Cycle plus 3 years under ADR 0050's rule for records that belong to no single VA Submission (everything before the next Cycle's first day), and `DisposalStore` deletes them with the Cycle.

Consequences:
- `RoleHolders` joins the classes `ArchitectureTest.onlyNamedClassesWriteRoleGrants` allows; `IdentityStatus` joins the boundary ports only adapters implement.
- `Identity` has an eighth component, `SignInProperties` a seventh, `RoleGrant` three more (`suspensionCause`, `returnedAt`, `returnTrigger`); `SessionLimitsFilter` takes the session check and `StepUp` the disablement; `Role.PER_SYSTEM` is the one list of the roles held for a System.
- A suspended grant does not come back by itself from a lapse: the return's update skips it, so neither a Register apply nor a Quarter's start lists it under the automatic returns; a restore leaves it lapsed until something returns it. A restore is refused in the same statement when the person is marked disabled, so a disablement that commits between the read and the write still wins.
- The test reset clears the suspension of the VA POCs' grants, every `idp_disabled_at` and the reviews.

Amends ADR 0008 (the session check is a third session limit) and ADR 0055 (`RoleLapse` also writes suspensions).
