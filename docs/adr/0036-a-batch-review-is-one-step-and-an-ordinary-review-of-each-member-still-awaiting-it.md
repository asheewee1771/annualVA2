# A batch review is one step and an ordinary review of each member still awaiting it

Status: accepted (items 3 and 4 decided 2026-10-25, see the end)

Ticket 29 (story 51, story 52 in part) lets the Cyber Manager confirm or Dispute a Bulk-set batch (ADR 0028) as a whole, and groups a batch's members under it on the review screen. It adds one migration (V22), two endpoints, two filters on the Findings list and no module: the batch review is in `finding` beside the single review (ADR 0035), and reuses its lock, its write and its segregation-of-duties check.

1. **A batch review is one step and an ordinary review per member.** `POST /api/batches/{id}/reviews` takes `decision` and `comment`, the body of a single review without `proposalId` or `justificationVersion` (either is a 400 `invalid-field` naming it). It writes one `batch_review` row (the batch, who and when) and, for each member it decides, a `finding_review` row that carries `batch_review_id`, with the decision and the comment, naming the batch's proposal of that Finding; each Finding takes the decision as its review state. It is the same shape as a Bulk-set: what was decided is on the Findings' reviews and is not repeated on the batch review. A review of one Finding is the same write with one Finding and no batch review (`ReviewStore.reviewFindings`, one `INSERT ... SELECT FROM unnest` and one `UPDATE`), so everything ADR 0035 guarantees for a review (the comment cleaned and bounded, required to Dispute, the review of a named proposal of the same Finding, the history kept) holds for every member by construction. The answer is 201 with the batch review (`id`, `batchId`, `decision`, `comment`, `reviewedBy`, `reviewedAt`), the Findings it decided (`reviewed`) and the members it left out (`leftOut`, item 3). `batch_review` is append-only by the trigger the reviews use (V21); ticket 49's retention deletes the Findings' reviews before the batch reviews.

2. **The checks are the single review's.** The batch is found first (one that does not exist is 404); then `Submissions.lockForReview` runs ownership (another System's Cyber Manager gets 404, ADR 0013), segregation of duties (`403 sod-conflict`) and the state (In Review, else `409 invalid-transition`), holding the VA Submission shared and recording the caller at step `REVIEW`; then the fields; then, with every member's Finding held `FOR UPDATE` in id order (`ReviewStore.lockBatch`, the order every proposal, Bulk-set and settlement locks them, ADR 0028 item 4), which members to decide. Refusals other than a 400 or 404 are `BATCH_REVIEWED` REFUSED entries with `batch_id` and a fixed `reason` (`sod-conflict`, `state-not-reviewable`, `nothing-to-review`), in a transaction of their own (ADR 0014). The batch review writes through `ReviewStore`, so `ArchitectureTest.writesOfAReviewTakeTheReviewLock` covers it with no change.

3. **Which members a batch review decides.** A member is decided only when its current proposal is still the batch's proposal of it and its review is `PENDING`. A batch's proposals never change, so such a member is exactly what the screen showed, and the request names nothing to compare (unlike `proposalId` for one Finding). Every other member is left out and listed with why: `PROPOSAL_CHANGED` (proposed again since, alone or in a later batch, or its proposal withdrawn by a rescan) or `REVIEWED` (already confirmed or Disputed, by a review of its own or an earlier review of the batch). So a Dispute or confirmation made one by one is never overwritten by a batch, and a member the SMO/Tech Lead changed after a return for rework is reviewed on its own. With no member to decide, the batch review is `409 nothing-to-review`, audited, and writes nothing.

4. **The audit is one batch entry and one entry per Finding.** A batch review is one `BATCH_REVIEWED` SUCCESS entry about the System and the VA Submission with `batch_id`, `count` (the Findings decided) and `decision`, and each Finding decided is also a `FINDING_REVIEWED` entry with `decision` and `batch_id`, as a Bulk-set writes a `STATUS_PROPOSED` entry per Finding (reviewer decisions, 2026-10-17), so an audit search by Finding shows a review made in a batch. Every key was already on the allow-list, so V22 changes no audit constraint. The comment is never audited or logged.

5. **Listing the batches.** `GET /api/submissions/{id}/batches` (anyone who may see the System; another System's VA Submission is 404) lists the batches in the order they were made, each with `batchId`, `proposedBy`, `proposedAt`, `proposal` (the proposal of its first member: the status and fields are those of every member, ADR 0028 item 1, and its `id` is that member's proposal) and the counts of its members: `count` (when it was made), `awaitingReview`, `confirmed` and `disputed` (still in the batch, by review state), `changedSince` (current proposal no longer the batch's) and `manuallyEntered` (members still in the batch entered by hand, story 52, so a batch holding one is marked before one Confirm batch decides it). No count is called `pending` (reviewer decisions, 2026-10-17). The proposal carries what the SMO/Tech Lead wrote, so every read is a `BATCHES_VIEWED` entry with the `count`, as a read of a Finding's proposals is.

6. **The Findings list narrows to a batch.** `GET /api/submissions/{id}/findings` takes `batchId` (the Findings whose current proposal was made in that batch: its members still in it) and `batched` (`true` for every Finding whose current proposal was made in some batch, `false` for the others, a single proposal or none). Both read the current proposal, so a member proposed again leaves its batch's group, and the total and pages are of the narrowed list.

7. **The screen.** `/cyber-manager/submissions/{id}` shows, after the not-applicable Scan Reports, a section of Bulk-set batches: each with its status and fields, its counts, a Manually Entered flag with the count when it holds any, Confirm batch and Dispute batch with one labelled and classified comment box while any member awaits review and the VA Submission is In Review, after a batch review the number of members left out and why, and its members still in it, collapsed until opened (so the screen reads a batch's members only when they are looked at), each with its own Confirm and Dispute. Then the Findings not in a batch, as before. Manually Entered Findings stay flagged wherever they are listed (story 52). The parts of the screen share one review session (a React context: the VA Submission, whether it is reviewable, the classification, and one refresh key); every review bumps the key and every read on the screen (`useResource`'s `refreshKey`) is made again, since a batch review changes its members and a member reviewed alone changes its batch's counts.

Not done here, on purpose: re-review of only what changed after a return (ticket 32), return for rework and pass to endorsement (ticket 30), and any Playwright scenario (ticket 53).

Decided 2026-10-25 by the user, as built (docs/agents/reviewer-decisions.md):
- **Item 3:** members already reviewed one by one are left out of a later batch review, so a batch confirmed by mistake is corrected Finding by Finding. The ticket's "is PENDING" says so; the alternative is to let a batch review re-decide every member whose current proposal is still the batch's.
- **Item 4:** the ticket says "audited as one batch review"; the per-Finding entries are added by analogy with the 2026-10-17 decision for Bulk-set. The alternative is the `BATCH_REVIEWED` entry alone.

Amends ADR 0035 item 4 (a Finding's review may name the batch review it was made in) and ADR 0028 (the Findings list filters by batch, and a batch has a list endpoint).
