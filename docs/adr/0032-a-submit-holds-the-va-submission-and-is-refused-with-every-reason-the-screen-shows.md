# A submit holds the VA Submission, stores the Declaration it accepted, and is refused with every reason the screen shows

Status: accepted

Ticket 25 (stories 40 and 41) lets the SMO/Tech Lead tick the fixed Declaration and submit a VA Submission for review. It adds one migration (V19), two endpoints and no module. The command is in `submission` (the state machine, ADR 0020); what stands in the way is worked out in `finding`, which keeps the Findings and the Scan Reports, and `ingestion`, which keeps the files, behind ports so the modules keep their downward order. The Declaration wording is a placeholder until ticket 24 agrees it.

1. **The Declaration is versioned wording, seeded, and never changed.** `declaration_text` holds one row per version: `version`, `text` (1 to 4000 characters) and `placeholder`. The current wording is the highest version. V19 seeds version 1, marked as a placeholder in the table and in its first words ("PLACEHOLDER WORDING, NOT YET AGREED."). Ticket 24 adds the agreed wording as version 2 with `placeholder` false by a new migration: no code changes. A trigger refuses UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE on it, so a version once shown is never rewritten. `GET /api/declaration-text` (SMO/Tech Lead, Cyber Manager, VA POC) answers `version`, `text` and `placeholder`; it is no one's data and is not audited.
2. **What was accepted is a snapshot.** Each successful submit adds a `declaration` row: the VA Submission, the version, a copy of its text (copied by the same statement, from that version), who accepted it and when. A VA Submission returned for rework and submitted again has one row per submit; the table is append-only like the proposals (V14), and ticket 49 replaces its trigger. `va_submission` gains `submitted_at` and `submitted_by`, written at each submit. `GET /api/submissions/{id}` now also answers `submittedAt` and `declaration` (the latest: `version`, `text`, `acceptedBy`, `acceptedAt`), null until the first submit.
3. **`POST /api/submissions/{id}/submit`** (SMO/Tech Lead) takes `declarationAccepted` and `declarationVersion` and answers 200 with the VA Submission In Review. Checks run in the order ownership (another System's VA Submission or one that does not exist is 404, ADR 0013; another role is 403), then, with the VA Submission's row held (item 5), the state (Draft or Returned for Rework, else 409 `invalid-transition`, audited `REFUSED` with `reason=state-not-submittable`), then everything that stands in the way and the Declaration, listed together. The Declaration is accepted only when `declarationAccepted` is true and `declarationVersion` is the current version: not ticked is `declaration-not-accepted`, and ticked against another version (or none) is `declaration-changed`, so the wording stored is the one the person read. A refusal for any of these is one 409 `not-ready` whose `reasons` lists each as `{reason, message, reportType, files}`: a fixed code, a fixed sentence (a count at most), the Scan Report for `report-missing`, and the files it is about as `{fileId, name, reportType}` in upload order. It is audited `REFUSED` with `reason=not-ready` and the `count` of reasons. On success, in one transaction: the Declaration row, the state `IN_REVIEW` with `submitted_at/by`, the caller at step `SUBMIT` (kept from the first time), a `SUBMISSION_SUBMITTED` entry with `state_from`, `state_to` and `declaration_version` (V19 adds the key), and a `SubmissionEvent` (ids, the two states, the actor and the instant) published for the notification tickets, which listen after the commit, so a refused or rolled-back submit publishes nothing. Segregation of duties has only the submitting step so far, so the submit records it and checks nothing more; the review tickets add the check.
4. **What stands in the way is worked out once, in a fixed order, and the screen reads the same list.** `SubmitChecks` (in `finding`, implementing `submission.SubmitReadiness`) gives, in order: `files-scanning` (any file, supporting evidence and a rescan included, not yet parsed, ready or rejected: reviewer decisions, "A VA Submission cannot leave Draft or Returned for Rework while any of its files is scanning"); `report-missing` for each Scan Report in turn that is uploaded but holds no live report file whose Findings were read (Secure Configuration's sentence says it is always required; a Scan Report not applicable or entered by hand always has its justification, ADR 0030, and counts as provided, with or without Findings); `findings-unproposed` (story 40, with the count); and the age rule (ADR 0031): `files-too-old`, `files-undated` and `files-future-dated` (a live file whose judged date is after today, which only a date that was today's when it was read can be). The files come from `finding.ReportFileReadiness` (it replaces `ReportFileAges`; `ingestion.FileReadiness` implements it), one read of the VA Submission's files. `GET /api/submissions/{id}/readiness` gains `blockers`, the same list without the Declaration, which the screen ticks; `tooOld` and `undated` stay. The Target Date Missed block arrives with Release 2.
5. **A submit holds the VA Submission's row; every write to what the SMO/Tech Lead prepares holds it shared.** The submit takes `va_submission` `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` before it reads the state and the reasons; a proposal, a Bulk-set, a Finding entered by hand, a mode change, a scan-date confirmation and the keeping of an upload or a rescan take it `FOR SHARE` and read the state under it. This is one primitive, `Submissions.lockForEdit(id, onRefused)`: it checks ownership, takes the lock, refuses a VA Submission not in Draft or Returned for Rework through the caller's own `Refusal` (which audits it), and returns an `EditTarget`, a type only it can make (private constructor). Every statement that writes prepared content takes an `EditTarget` (`ReportFileStore.insert` and `confirm`, `ScanReportStore.lock`, `set` and `insertManual`, `Proposals.lock`, `fields` and `newBatch`), so a new write path cannot skip the lock, and `ArchitectureTest.writesToPreparedContentTakeTheEditLock` keeps it so. The submit runs the same check with the lock taken exclusively. Reads take `Submissions.readTarget` or `systemOf`, which grant nothing to write; the old `fileTarget(id, need)` is gone. So a write that started first finishes before the submit judges anything (a scan-date confirmation cannot race the age rule, ticket 23's note), and a write that comes after waits and then sees In Review and is refused. An upload is checked early by `Submissions.precheckEdit` (the same ownership and state, without the lock, so a refused upload is never quarantined; only `ReportFiles` may call it, and its `keep` must take `lockForEdit`, both checked by the same architecture test) and again when it is kept, under the lock; a refusal there discards the bytes, as every refusal after quarantine does. `NO KEY` lets the scan job keep inserting rows that refer to the VA Submission meanwhile. The scan job's settlement does not take the lock: the files are read first, so either a file is still being checked (and the submit is refused) or its settlement has committed and its Findings are counted. Shared locks are taken before any Finding or Scan Report lock, and the submit takes no other lock, so no lock order is reversed. Seam 1 runs one request at a time, so no test can show the lock; it is held by the statements and this ADR (as ADR 0030 item 6).
6. **The states.** `SubmissionState` gains `IN_REVIEW` and `RETURNED_FOR_REWORK` (the return commands are tickets 30 and 35; the tests arrange it). The SMO inbox shows In Review under Waiting on others, its next action `AWAIT_REVIEW` due on the Review Window's end, and Returned for Rework under Needs your action like a Draft.
7. **The screen.** The Declaration card shows the current wording as text with its version, a "Placeholder wording, not yet agreed" flag while it is one, and a classified tick box. Submit is disabled until the readiness has no blockers and the box is ticked; each blocker is listed with its sentence and the files it names. A refusal shows the server's problem and its reasons, and the wording is read again and must be ticked again. The readiness is asked for again when a file finishes its checks or a Scan Report's mode changes, as well as after proposals and confirmations. Once submitted the screen is read-only, decided once: the prepare screen provides `EditableContext` from the state, and every control that changes the VA Submission (the mode, manual-entry, upload, Replace and scan-date forms, the Findings' tick boxes, status selects and proposal form, select-all and the bulk bar) sits in an `<Editable>`, a fieldset disabled while the VA Submission is not editable. Downloads, filters and paging sit outside it. The Declaration card then shows when it was submitted and the wording accepted. The screen owns the one readiness read, which the Findings and the Declaration share; it is read again after a proposal, a Finding entered by hand, a scan-date confirmation, a refused submit, and whenever the files or the Scan Reports' modes are read again.

Not done here, on purpose: the agreed Declaration wording and its owner (ticket 24), the Target Date Missed block (Release 2), the segregation-of-duties check at the review step (ticket 28), the notifications that consume the event (tickets 36 and 37), and any Playwright scenario (ticket 53).

Consequences: every write to a VA Submission's prepared content now also locks its row shared, which is cheap and never waits except on a submit; a Burp Suite file needs its scan date given before submitting (ADR 0031); the readiness read and the submit refuse with the same reasons, so the screen and the server never disagree about what is left to do.

Amends ADR 0020 (the submit is the second command; the VA Submission read gains `submittedAt` and `declaration`), ADR 0028 item 6 and ADR 0031 item 6 (the readiness read gains `blockers`; `ReportFileAges` becomes `ReportFileReadiness`), and ADR 0030 item 6 (a write to a Scan Report locks the VA Submission shared before the Scan Report).

Amended by ADR 0033: a submit keeps the Overdue flag only if the Review Window has passed as well.

Amended by the decision of 2026-10-24 (`docs/agents/reviewer-decisions.md`): item 1's "by a new migration" no longer holds. A VA POC publishes each new Declaration version in the app (ticket 61); the table, the append-only trigger and "the current wording is the highest version" are unchanged.

Amended by ADR 0035: a submit checks segregation of duties after ownership and with the row held, before the state: someone who acted at, or is assigned to, the reviewing step (the System's Cyber Manager) is refused with `403 sod-conflict`, audited `SUBMISSION_SUBMITTED` REFUSED with `reason=sod-conflict`. Item 3's "the review tickets add the check" is done.

Amended by ticket 61 (the user's decisions of 2026-11-22): one VA POC publishes a version with a fresh sign-in (step-up intent `declaration-publish`), with no second approver, and it is current at once, with no effective date. V46 adds `published_by` and `published_at` to `declaration_text` (null for the placeholder V19 seeded); a publish inserts the next version, never a placeholder, under an advisory lock so two publishes never take the same number. The wording is cleaned and bounded by the one rule for long text (ADR 0027 item 5), and a refused wording is audited `DECLARATION_PUBLISHED` REFUSED with `reason=invalid-field`; a publish is audited with `declaration_version` only. `GET /api/declaration-text/versions` (VA POC) lists every version with its publisher, whether it is current or superseded and the count of VA Submissions that accepted it; like the current wording, it is no one's data and is not audited. Item 3's `declaration-changed` is how an SMO/Tech Lead with the older wording open learns of a new version.
