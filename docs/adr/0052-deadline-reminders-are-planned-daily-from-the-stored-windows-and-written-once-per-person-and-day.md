# Deadline reminders are planned daily from the stored windows and written once per person and day

Status: accepted (items 3, 4 and 5 decided 2026-10-25, docs/agents/reviewer-decisions.md)

Ticket 38 (stories 88, 89 and 90) reminds the SMO/Tech Lead, the Cyber Manager and the VA POCs of their deadlines by email and in the app. It adds one migration (`V149_42_38`), a read of waiting work in `submission`, a planner and its job in `notification`, ten `NoticeAction`s and four mail templates, and no endpoint.

1. **A planner over the stored state.** `DeadlineReminders.plan` (in `notification`, which owns the reminder schedule) reads the clock once, takes today's business date in `Asia/Singapore` and asks `WaitingWork.on(today)` (in `submission`, which owns windows and Overdue) for every piece of work that waits on a window: each VA Submission whose state has a `current_window_due` (V20), and each System with none in the Cycle (Not Started, due by `NOT_STARTED`'s window for its Quarter). Each comes with the step whose person acts next (`SubmissionState.nextStep`: Not Started, Draft and Returned for Rework submit, In Review reviews, Awaiting Endorsement endorses), its due date by that one rule, and the business day the sweep flagged it (ADR 0033: the flag is read, never computed). `WaitingWork` looks where the sweep looks, through one `OpenQuarters` both use: every Quarter not Handled outside the app of every VA Cycle that has not closed, with the active Systems that Cycle holds in it (`RegisterSystems.everyActive(year)`, past the access guard). So a retired System (whose VA Submission is Withdrawn), the final states and the frozen Exemption Requested, which wait on no window, are never reminded; the close of a VA Cycle makes what is left of it final. `ArchitectureTest` lets only `OpenQuarters`, the sweep and the lifecycle list every System, and only the planner use `WaitingWork`.

2. **The schedule, written once.** `DeadlineReminders.schedule(step)` is the one table: for each step, the person told (as the Overdue notice of ADR 0043 item 3 addresses them), the link to their work, the reminder for each number of days left, and the weekly reminder while Overdue with its copy:
   - submitting (the SMO/Tech Lead) and reviewing (the Cyber Manager): 5 days before the due date (`SUBMIT_DUE_SOON`, `REVIEW_DUE_SOON`), on it (`…_DUE_TODAY`), and every 7 days after the business day the sweep set the flag, while it stays set (`…_STILL_OVERDUE`). The sweep's own Overdue message goes on the day it sets the flag, so the weekly series starts a week after it and never repeats it, whenever the work became Overdue (work flagged late, a flag kept across a submit, or a System added late, item 6). Returned for Rework waits on the Review Window (ADR 0033 item 2), so its SMO/Tech Lead is reminded against the end of week 4, not week 2.
   - endorsing (both VA POCs): 14 and 7 days before the Quarter's last day (`ENDORSE_DUE_IN_TWO_WEEKS`, `ENDORSE_DUE_IN_ONE_WEEK`), sent as a pair awaiting `ENDORSEMENT`, so either VA POC's endorsement or return clears both (story 69). No weekly reminder: story 90 asks for none, and the sweep's `ENDORSE_OVERDUE` tells them once.

3. **Copies go on the weekly Overdue reminders only** (decided 2026-10-25). Story 88 reads "then weekly while Overdue with the Cyber Manager copied in", and the implementation plan attaches the copy to the weekly message; story 89 makes the Cyber Manager "the one copied in on that System's SMO reminders". The planner sends the copy with each weekly reminder: the Cyber Manager for the SMO/Tech Lead's (`SUBMIT_OVERDUE_COPY`, linking their inbox, since a Draft is not theirs to open), each VA POC for the Cyber Manager's (`REVIEW_OVERDUE_COPY`, linking the VA Submission). The relay has no cc, so each copy is a notification and a mail of its own. Copying the 5-day and due-day reminders too is one more field in the schedule.

4. **A VA POC's copy is theirs alone** (decided 2026-10-25). The spec says items for the VA POCs clear for one when the other acts; a copy asks for no decision, so `Notifications.toEachVaPoc` writes one row each with no pair, and nothing clears it. Sending it as a pair awaiting `ENDORSEMENT` would clear it when the VA Submission is endorsed.

5. **Mail templates** (decided 2026-10-25, MCNS onboarding in ticket 57). Every reminder is a required message (spec, *Notifications*) and is mailed: `REMINDER_DUE_SOON` (the 5-day, 2-week and 1-week reminders), `REMINDER_DUE` (the last day), `OVERDUE_WEEKLY` and `REMINDER_COPY`, each with its own `annualva.mail.templates` id (`ANNUALVA_MAIL_TEMPLATE_*`). The `action` value carries the app's sentence, so one template serves every role; ticket 57's list names separate Cyber Manager and VA POC copy variants, which would be one more constant and id each. The values are ADR 0046's allow-list, nothing more.

6. **A System added after its window** (reviewer decisions, 2026-10-23). Until Release 2 gives an added System its own windows, the sweep flags a System added after its Quarter's Submission Window at once, so its SMO/Tech Lead gets the Overdue message straight away, then the weekly reminders from that day with the copy to its Cyber Manager, all due by its Quarter's dates. `va_system.added_on` is not used. Seam 1 proves it.

7. **Once per person and day, safe to run again.** A reminder `Notice` carries the business day it is for (`reminderOn`, set only by `Notice.reminder`, which also requires the due date). `notification` gains `reminder_on` with a partial unique index on `(recipient_id, action, system_id, due_on, reminder_on)` where it is set, and every insert is `ON CONFLICT … DO NOTHING`; a notice of a move has none and is unchanged. The mail is queued only when the row was written, so a second pass of the day, or a second instance's pass at the same time (which waits on the first's uncommitted row and then inserts nothing), writes and mails nothing more. This is the idempotency of ADR 0012 without a `scheduler_lock` row.

8. **When it runs, and failures.** `DeadlineReminderJob` runs the planner at 08:00 Singapore time and then every hour to the end of the day (`annualva.reminders.cron`, default `0 0 8-23 * * *`, zone `annualva.time-zone`) when scheduling is on. The first pass sends the day's reminders; the later ones write only what it could not (a failed write, or a morning the application was down). A reminder is for its own day, so the next day's passes never send a missed one late: a late "due in 5 days" would say the wrong thing, and the next reminder in the series still comes. Each piece of work is reminded in a transaction of its own, which first holds its VA Submission's state (`WaitingWork.stillWaiting`, `FOR SHARE`): a reminder is never about a state already left, and a VA POC pair it opens commits before a decision's commit clears that VA Submission's pairs. A piece that cannot be reminded leaves the rest to go out; a pass logs one `reminder-failed` line with the count and an exception type when any failed, and one `reminders-planned` line with the counts, never an address or a value (proven by a Seam 1 test that makes every notification write fail and then runs the planner again). A Seam 1 test moves the clock and calls `POST /api/test/jobs/reminders`.

Not done here, on purpose: reminders for Unlinked or Unreachable SMOs beyond what is sent to everyone (ticket 39); a meter for the passes (ticket 52); the weekly digest of FYIs (story 93, Release 2).

Consequences:
- A new reminder is a `NoticeAction`, its `MailTemplate.of` mapping, a row of the schedule, and a migration widening `notification_action_check` that sorts after `V149_42_38` and keeps the union.
- Every deployed profile needs the four new template ids; `sit` has placeholders until ticket 57.
- The reminders read what the sweep stored, so a weekly reminder waits for the sweep to flag the work (it runs hourly, so on the reminder's day it has).

Amends ADR 0033 (the sweep and the reminders look in the same open Quarters, and the flag's day drives the weekly reminder), ADR 0043 (the `Notifications` port writes a reminder at most once per person and day, and `toEachVaPoc` sends an item to each VA POC that no decision clears) and ADR 0046 item 2 (the reminders' four templates).
