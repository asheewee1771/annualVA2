# The rework queue is the needs-attention Findings in stages, for the SMO/Tech Lead alone

Status: accepted (items 2, 3 and 6 decided 2026-10-25, docs/agents/reviewer-decisions.md)

Ticket 31 (stories 60, 61 and 62) gives the SMO/Tech Lead of a VA Submission Returned for Rework a guided queue: one Finding at a time, Disputed first, with the Cyber Manager's Dispute comment, keys 1 to 4 to pick a status and Enter to save, then Resubmit. It adds one endpoint, one audit action, one screen and no migration. The read lives in `finding` (`ReworkQueues`, `ReworkQueueStore`), which owns the Findings, their proposals and their reviews.

1. **One rule for what needs the SMO/Tech Lead.** The queue holds exactly the Findings the Findings list calls "needs attention": no proposal yet, or a Disputed one (`FindingStore.NEEDS_ATTENTION`, now written once and read by both). A new proposal on a Disputed Finding makes it the current proposal and puts its review back to `PENDING` (ADR 0027 item 2, unchanged), so it leaves the queue at once; a confirmed Finding stays confirmed and is never in it (ADR 0039 item 2). The resubmit is the ordinary `POST /api/submissions/{id}/submit` (ADR 0032), which moves Returned for Rework back to In Review; nothing new is needed for it.

2. **Stages, in queue order** (decided 2026-10-25 on the reading of "pending"): `DISPUTED`, then `NEW_CRITICAL_HIGH`, then `NEW_MEDIUM_LOW` (Medium, Low and Informational), as `ReworkStage` names them; Release 2 puts Target Date Missed and Carried Over (to re-confirm) between the first and the second. The ticket's "new Critical/High pending" is read as a Finding with no proposal yet, the only new Finding that needs the SMO/Tech Lead (one proposed and awaiting review needs the Cyber Manager); this follows the reviewer decision of 2026-10-17 that no count is called a bare "pending". Within a stage the order is the Findings list's: the most severe first, then by identifier, package or rule, and arrival (`FindingStore.SEVERITY_FIRST`). The stage is computed in the query, so the order and the counts come from one expression.

3. **Only the System's SMO/Tech Lead reads it** (decided 2026-10-25). `GET /api/submissions/{id}/rework-queue` is the SMO/Tech Lead's work list: the role rule refuses a Cyber Manager, a VA POC or an Auditor (`403`), and the access guard on `Need.SUBMIT` answers `404` for an SMO/Tech Lead of another System, as for one that does not exist (ADR 0013). It reads in any state (a read model, like the Findings list); the screen offers it once the VA Submission is Returned for Rework. A Cyber Manager reads the same Findings through the Findings list.

4. **The answer.** `{stages: [{stage, count}], items: [{stage, finding, dispute}], page, size, total}`: every stage with its count, an empty one included; `finding` is the Findings list's view (the current proposal, the review state and the latest review); `dispute` is the Cyber Manager's latest review of the Finding when it Disputed, else null, so for a Disputed Finding it is the Dispute of the current proposal and why. Pages are as the Findings list's (`page`, `size` up to 500). A Finding leaves the queue when it is proposed again, so the screen reads the first page again after each save and the next Finding takes the saved one's place.

5. **Audit.** The queue carries what the Cyber Manager and the SMO/Tech Lead wrote, so every read is a `REWORK_QUEUE_VIEWED` entry about the System and the VA Submission with the `count` returned, the owner's included (reviewer decision of 2026-10-14: a content-bearing read is audited for every role). No detail key is new, so no migration.

6. **The screen** (`/smo/submissions/{id}/rework`; the SMO inbox and the prepare screen lead to it once Returned for Rework). It shows why the VA Submission came back, from `GET /api/submissions/{id}/returns` and the Scan Reports read: the latest return's comment as text, and each not-applicable justification it Disputed with the Dispute comment and the wording Disputed. Rewriting a justification stays on the prepare screen, which the rework screen links to (decided 2026-10-25: the queue is of Findings, as the ticket says). Then the stages with what is left in each (the current one marked), a visible key hint, and one Finding at a time: what it is, the Dispute as text, what is proposed now, and the four statuses as buttons bound to keys 1 to 4 (`aria-keyshortcuts`). Picking the status already proposed starts from what was written. A key typed in a field is text: digits pick only outside a field, Enter saves only outside a field, button or link (Enter on a button activates it), and Esc leaves a field so the keys work again; a key with Ctrl, Alt or Cmd is left to the browser. Previous and Skip move without saving. The Declaration and Resubmit are the prepare screen's form (`DeclarationForm`, now shared), labelled Resubmit once Returned for Rework.

Not done here, on purpose:
- Target Date Missed and Carried Over stages (Release 2).
- Re-review of only what changed after the resubmit (ticket 32) and the notifications (tickets 36 and 37).

Consequences:
- The Findings list and the rework queue cannot disagree about what needs the SMO/Tech Lead.
- The keyboard rules are not covered by an automated test (no front-end unit tests, ADR 0009); the end-to-end scenario of the rework queue belongs to the e2e suite (spec, "a few end-to-end scenarios ... the rework queue").
