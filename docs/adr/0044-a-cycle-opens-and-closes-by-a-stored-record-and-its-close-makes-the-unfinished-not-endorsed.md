# A VA Cycle opens and closes by a stored record, and its close makes everything unfinished Not Endorsed

Status: accepted (items 4 and 7 provisional, awaiting a human decision)

Ticket 41 (stories 17 and 68) gives the VA Cycle its lifecycle: it opens when its Q1 starts, overlaps the older Cycle for up to 4 weeks, and closes 4 weeks after its Q4 ends, or earlier when a VA POC closes it, making every VA Submission that is neither Endorsed nor Exempted Not Endorsed. It adds one migration (V141), one endpoint, one scheduled job and no module. The calendar stays computed (ADR 0015); only what cannot be derived is stored, as ADR 0015 item 1 and the reviewer decision of 2026-10-05 foresaw.

1. **What is stored.** `va_cycle` (written by the cycle module's `CycleStore`) holds one row per Cycle the lifecycle opened: `opened_at`, and once it closes `closed_at`, `closed_by` (the VA POC; null for the job) and `closes_on`, an early close's business date overriding the default (Q4's end plus 28 days). `CycleCalendar` reads the closes into `VaCycle.closedAt` and `VaCycle.closesOn`. ADR 0015 item 3's rule changes in one place, `VaCycle.phaseOn`: a Cycle is `CLOSED` once its close is recorded **or** the day after its close date, whichever comes first, so nothing is accepted between midnight and the job's pass that records the close. `VaCycle.openOn` (and `open` on every Cycle `GET /api/cycles` lists, with `closedAt`) is `RUNNING` or `CLOSING`.

2. **Opening.** `CycleLifecycleJob` runs `CycleLifecycle.run` at startup and then an hour after each pass (`annualva.cycle.lifecycle-interval`, default `1h`) when `annualva.scheduling.enabled` is true: the ticket's "daily job", run hourly as the Overdue sweep is (ADR 0033 item 6), so an opening or a close lands within the hour after midnight in Singapore and a pass that finds nothing to do changes nothing. A pass records the opening of every Cycle whose Q1 has started (`CYCLE_OPENED`, no actor, with the `cycle`; once per Cycle) and keeps the older Cycles' Systems (item 3). A new Cycle takes its Systems and Quarters from the standing Register, which is already how every reader lists them.

3. **While two Cycles overlap a Register apply changes only the newer one.** The register module keeps the Register as an older Cycle saw it (`cycle_register`, `cycle_register_system`: each System active then, with its Quarter) from the moment a newer Cycle starts. `RegisterSystems.keepOlderCycles` does it, under the Register apply's advisory lock, for every Cycle that is no longer the newest to have started and is not kept yet; the job calls it and **every apply calls it first**, so an apply on the newer Cycle's first day, before the job's pass, still leaves the older Cycle as it was. Every reader of a Cycle's Systems asks `RegisterSystems.active(need, cycleYear)` or, for a job, `everyActive(cycleYear)`: the dashboards (`DashboardSystems`), the Overdue sweep's Not Started rows (amends ADR 0033 item 3: by the System's Quarter in that Cycle), the close, and the start, which takes the Quarter from the Cycle and refuses a System not in it (`409 invalid-transition`, reason `not-in-cycle`: added after the newer Cycle opened). Only membership and the Quarter are kept. Who may see or act on a System is still the access guard's answer from the Register as it is (ADR 0013), and a System retired since is left out of every read and job as before (reviewer decisions, 2026-10-23); what a transfer or a retirement does to in-flight work in either Cycle is the Register-effects ticket's (43). `ArchitectureTest.onlyTheScheduledJobsListSystemsPastTheAccessGuard` lets only `OverdueSweep` and `CycleLifecycle` call `everyActive`.

4. **Who closes, and when** (provisional). `POST /api/cycles/{year}/close` (role `VA_POC`, body `{}`) is a step-up action (intent `cycle-close`, the spec's "closing a Cycle early"), checked before anything is read. A year the app does not run is `404`. Then, with the Cycle held (item 6), it is refused while the Cycle's Q4 is still running (`409 invalid-transition`, reason `cycle-running`) and once it is closed (reason `cycle-closed`), each a `CYCLE_CLOSED` REFUSED entry with the `cycle`. **A VA POC may close a Cycle only after its Q4 has ended**: "earlier" is read as earlier than the 4-week grace for late Q4 endorsements (story 17), not as ending a Cycle whose Quarters are still running, which would make every Q4 System Not Endorsed before its Submission Window. Closed before its close date, the Cycle's `closes_on` becomes today; closed after it (the job not having run yet), the date is kept. The job closes, for nobody, every Cycle past its close date not yet closed. The controller lives in `submission`, beside the commands, because the close moves VA Submissions; the cycle module, which may not depend on the submissions or the audit log, keeps only the reads and the record.

5. **What the close does**, in one transaction, for the Cycle's Quarters the app runs (never one Handled outside the app):
   - every VA Submission of an active System in a state that is not final (`SubmissionState.isFinal`, now the one rule `SystemState.isFinal` asks too) becomes `NOT_ENDORSED`, Exemption Requested included (spec *Release 1 decisions*); the rows are held in id order as a move holds them, so the close waits for a command in progress and leaves alone what that command endorsed meanwhile. The UPDATE sets `SubmissionStore.OVERDUE_AFTER_MOVE`, which clears the Overdue flag (a final state waits on no window);
   - every active System of the Cycle with no VA Submission (Not Started) gets one, `NOT_ENDORSED`, with its Quarter's windows. Nobody started it, so V141 lets `started_at` and `started_by` be null, on such a row only (a CHECK); `Submission.startedAt` is nullable. The `not_started_overdue` row stays as the record of the earlier flag;
   - the close is recorded (item 1);
   - each change is a `SUBMISSION_NOT_ENDORSED` entry about the System and the VA Submission with `state_from` (`NOT_STARTED` for a System), `state_to` and the `cycle`, and the close one `CYCLE_CLOSED` entry with the `cycle` and the `count`; all are attributed to the VA POC who closed it, or to nobody for the job (reviewer decisions, 2026-10-18: a change made as a consequence of a person's action is theirs). V141 adds `cycle` to the audit detail keys;
   - one `CycleClosed` event is published, naming every VA Submission made Not Endorsed with the state it was in (null for Not Started) and who closed it. The notifications (ADR 0043) listen after the commit: each System's SMO/Tech Lead gets a `NOT_ENDORSED` notice linked to the VA Submission (V141 widens `notification_action_check`), and a VA POC's close also clears the endorsement pair of a VA Submission that was Awaiting Endorsement, as an endorsement would; the job's close acts for nobody, so its pairs stay open. Mail follows with ticket 37. The answer is `{year, closesOn, closedAt, notEndorsed}`.

   VA Submissions of retired Systems are left to the Register's withdraw (ticket 43), as every job leaves them.

6. **After the close no transition is accepted.** One check, `Submissions.requireOpenCycle`, refuses with `409 invalid-transition` and an entry with reason `cycle-closed` whenever the VA Submission's Cycle is `CLOSED` by item 1: in `atStep` (every review, return, pass and endorsement, and the later step commands), in the submit and in the start. After the close every unfinished VA Submission is Not Endorsed anyway, so the state checks refuse too; the check matters between the close date and the job's pass, and for a start, which has no row to hold. The one exception the spec allows, a Q4 conditional Exemption's VA recorded, confirmed or lapsed after the close, is ticket 42's command, which does not ask this check. Edits to prepared content (uploads, proposals) are not transitions and are refused by their state once the close has run. **Concurrency:** a start holds its Cycle shared and a close holds it exclusively (a transaction-scoped advisory lock keyed by the year, because a Cycle has no row before its Q1), so no Draft lands in a Cycle while it closes and a close sees every start before it. The locks are always taken in the order Register apply lock, Cycle lock, VA Submission rows, so the job, a close and an apply cannot deadlock.

7. **The SMO inbox lists Not Endorsed under Done, labelled as a failure** (provisional; the reviewer decision of 2026-10-24 left it to this ticket). Nothing more can be done once the Cycle has closed, so the heading the server answers stays `DONE` with no next action, but the screen says "Not endorsed: the VA Cycle closed before it was endorsed" with a Not Endorsed flag instead of "Nothing more to do". The alternative is a heading of its own (a fourth `InboxGroup`), which every inbox would then have to place.

8. **The shell's Cycle selector.** The front end reads `GET /api/cycles` once in the shell (`CycleChoiceProvider`); while two Cycles are `open` the header shows one VA Cycle selector, and its choice is the `cycle` of every dashboard call (the SMO and Cyber Manager inboxes, the Quarter boards, the Cycle matrix), replacing each screen's own selector. A choice of a Cycle that has since closed falls back to the Cycle running today. The Cycle matrix offers the close, with a confirm dialog, while the shown Cycle is `CLOSING`.

Consequences: a Cycle's report is final once it closes; every reader of a Cycle's Systems goes through `RegisterSystems`; a new step command gets the closed-Cycle refusal by going through `atStep`; tests run a pass through `POST /api/test/jobs/cycle-lifecycle` after moving the clock, and the test reset empties the three new tables.

Amends ADR 0015 items 1 and 3, ADR 0019 (the SMO inbox's Not Endorsed label) and ADR 0033 item 3 (Not Started by the Cycle's own Quarter).
