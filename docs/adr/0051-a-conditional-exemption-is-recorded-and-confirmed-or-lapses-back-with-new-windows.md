# A conditional Exemption is recorded and confirmed, or lapses back with new windows

Status: accepted (items 2 to 7 decided 2026-10-25, items 8 and 9 decided 2026-10-26, docs/agents/reviewer-decisions.md)

Ticket 42 (story 47) finishes the conditional Exemption that ticket 40 approves (ADR 0048 item 5): its SMO/Tech Lead records that the scheduled VA happened and a VA POC confirms it; if nothing is recorded within 3 months after the Quarter's last day, a daily job lapses it. It adds one migration (V37), two endpoints, one scheduled job, a dashboard flag and no module. Everything is in `submission`, which owns the state machine, the Cycle close and segregation of duties.

1. **What is stored.** `exemption` gains, each written once: `va_happened_on`, `va_evidence`, `va_recorded_by`, `va_recorded_at` (the record), `confirmed_by`, `confirmed_at` (the confirmation) and `lapsed_at` (the lapse). CHECKs keep them coherent: only an Exemption with a lapse date (approved on a scheduled VA, V33) is recorded, confirmed or lapsed; a confirmation needs a record; a recorded one never lapses. An Exemption is conditional while it has a lapse date and is neither confirmed nor lapsed (`ExemptionStore.Row.conditional`, the one rule the answers and the reads use). Whether a VA Submission's conditional Exemption ever lapsed is read from the table (`exemption_lapsed`, an `EXISTS` in `SubmissionStore`'s columns), never stored twice.

2. **Record** (decided 2026-10-25 in the evidence). `POST /api/exemptions/{id}/va-happened` (role `SMO`; body `{vaDate, evidence}`) answers `200` with the Exemption. The checks, in order: the Exemption (`404` for an unknown id); `atStep` with a `RECORDING` gate (step `SUBMIT`, from Exempted only, `409 invalid-transition` with `state-not-exempted`), so ownership (`404` for another System's), segregation of duties (`403 sod-conflict`) and the row held exclusively; then, read again with the row held, that it is conditional (`not-conditional` for a prior VA), not recorded yet (`already-recorded`, also when the write-once `UPDATE` finds it recorded by a command that committed meanwhile) and its lapse date not passed (`exemption-lapsed`, also after `lapsesOn` before the job's pass, as the Cycle close refuses from the day after its close date, ADR 0044 item 1); then the fields (`ExemptionFields.happened`, IM8 as-1): `vaDate` falls in the scheduled VA's window (the day after the Quarter's last day to `lapsesOn`) and is not after today; `evidence` is long text by the one rule (`ReportText.cleanLongText`, required, at most 4,000 characters, the table checks the same), and every refusal names the field without echoing it. Audited as `EXEMPTION_VA_RECORDED` with the `exemption_id`, never the evidence; refusals with a `reason`. From then on the Exemption does not lapse (spec *Release 1 decisions*: "it doesn't lapse while that confirmation is pending"). Decided 2026-10-25: the form asks for evidence text as the request does, so the VA POC has something to confirm against; the ticket names only the date. A file could be attached later through the evidence pipeline as ADR 0048 item 3 does.

3. **Confirm** (decided 2026-10-25 in the step-up intent). `POST /api/exemptions/{id}/confirm-va` (role `VA_POC`, body `{}`) is a step-up action (spec: "every Exemption decision (approve, reject, confirm a conditional Exemption's VA)"), checked before anything is read, with the one intent `exemption-decide` that approving and rejecting use, so the front end's step-up explanation and return work unchanged. Then the Exemption (`404`), `atStep` with a `CONFIRMING` gate (step `ENDORSE`, as deciding, ADR 0048 item 5; from Exempted), so `403 sod-conflict` for a VA POC the Register names as the System's SMO/Tech Lead or Cyber Manager or who acted at another step; then that a VA is recorded (`va-not-recorded`) and not confirmed (`already-confirmed`, written once by `UPDATE ... WHERE confirmed_at IS NULL`). The answer has `conditional: false`. Audited as `EXEMPTION_VA_CONFIRMED`. A VA POC who approved the Exemption may confirm it: both are the endorsing step. Neither command moves the state, so neither goes through `Submissions.moved`; each publishes an `ExemptionEvent` for the notifications.

4. **After the Cycle closes** (decided 2026-10-25). ADR 0044 item 6's one exception is built as a gate that does not ask `Submissions.requireOpenCycle`: `Submissions.Gate.afterClose`, used by `RECORDING` and `CONFIRMING` only; every other gate still refuses once the Cycle is closed. It is not limited to Q4: a Q1 to Q3 Exemption cannot be recorded after its Cycle closes anyway (its lapse date is always before the close), but one recorded just before the close can still be confirmed after it, rather than stay conditional for ever. The commands touch only the Exemption, never the VA Submission's state, so the Cycle's report does not change. The alternative, refusing the Q1 to Q3 confirmation after the close, leaves such an Exemption conditional with no way to resolve it.

5. **The lapse.** `ExemptionLapse.run` (job `ExemptionLapseJob`, at startup and an hour after each pass, `annualva.exemption.lapse-interval`, default `1h`, when `annualva.scheduling.enabled`; the ticket's daily job run hourly as the Overdue sweep is, ADR 0033 item 6) reads the clock once. It holds every Cycle the app runs shared first, in year order (the lock a start takes, so no close runs meanwhile and the order Cycle, then rows, of ADR 0044 item 6 holds), reads each Cycle's phase again with its close as recorded, and claims, per Cycle, the Exempted VA Submissions of active Systems whose conditional Exemption has nothing recorded, has not lapsed and whose `lapses_on` is before today, in id order with `FOR NO KEY UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` (a record or a confirmation under way is skipped until the next pass, ADR 0033 item 5). Each is lapsed (`lapsed_at`, once) and:
   - while the Cycle is open (not `CLOSED` by `VaCycle.phaseOn`), it goes back to the state the request froze (`previous_state`; `NOT_STARTED` when the request created it, which the inbox's Start then takes, ADR 0048 item 4) with new windows: `submit_due` today plus 13 days, `review_due` 14 days after that, `endorse_due` the later of the Quarter's last day and the Cycle's close date (story 47). The windows must end in order (V8's `va_submission_windows_check`), and in a year whose Q4 is 13 weeks long a Q3 lapse's Review Window would end after the close (VA Cycle 2028: flagged 6 February 2029, review due 5 March, close 4 March), so a window that would end on or after the next one's last day is cut to end the day before it (decided 2026-10-25: the close ends every window anyway, and the SMO/Tech Lead keeps the full two weeks to submit whenever the close allows it; the alternative, an endorsement due after the close, shows a date the close overrides); the Overdue flag goes through `OVERDUE_AFTER_MOVE` (Exempted keeps none, so it is clear) and the sweep flags it when a new window passes. The flag the request froze (`previous_overdue_since`) is not put back: the windows are new;
   - once the Cycle has closed (always for Q4, whose lapse date is 3 months after the Quarter and so after the close), it becomes Not Endorsed through the same `MOVED` statement as any move, with its windows unchanged.

   Each lapse is an `EXEMPTION_LAPSED` entry for nobody with `state_from` `EXEMPTED`, `state_to` and the `exemption_id`. A second pass finds nothing. VA Submissions of retired Systems are left to the Register's withdraw, as every job leaves them; `ArchitectureTest.onlyTheScheduledJobsListSystemsPastTheAccessGuard` lets `ExemptionLapse` call `RegisterSystems.everyActive`.

6. **Notifications** (ADR 0043; decided 2026-10-25 in the channel). One `ExemptionEvent` (kind `VA_RECORDED`, `VA_CONFIRMED` or `LAPSED`) is published in the transaction and heard after the commit by `WorkflowNotices`: a record asks both VA POCs, as a pair (`Awaits.VA_CONFIRMATION`), to confirm it (`CONFIRM_VA`, linked to the queue); a confirmation clears the pair and tells the SMO/Tech Lead (`VA_CONFIRMED`); a lapse tells the SMO/Tech Lead (`EXEMPTION_LAPSED`, linked by `Links.forSmo`, or `EXEMPTION_LAPSED_NOT_ENDORSED`) and, as if the VA Submission had just moved there, the Cyber Manager (`REVIEW`) when it is back In Review and both VA POCs (`ENDORSE`, a pair) when it is back Awaiting Endorsement. All four new actions are in the app only, not mailed (`MailTemplate.of`): the spec's required messages do not name them, and the new Submission Window is mailed by the deadline reminders (ticket 38) and, once it passes, as Overdue. The alternative is mailing `EXEMPTION_LAPSED` with a template of its own, since it hands work back with a two-week window.

7. **The lapsed flag lasts** (decided 2026-10-25). Every dashboard row (SMO inbox, Cyber Manager inbox, Quarter board, Cycle matrix) answers `exemptionLapsed`, true once any conditional Exemption of the VA Submission lapsed, and the screens show "Exemption lapsed". It is a lasting mark like Endorsed Late, so a later endorsement, or a new Exemption, still shows that the first one was not honoured. The alternative is clearing it at the next request or move.

8. **Reads and screens** (the queue's scope decided 2026-10-26, kept as built: the confirmation queue reads every Cycle the app runs, docs/agents/reviewer-decisions.md). `GET /api/submissions/{id}` answers `conditionalExemption` (ids, dates and times only, no free text, so the owner's read stays unaudited, ADR 0020) for the SMO/Tech Lead's screen, which shows the VA happened form (date and evidence, each with its classification label, IM8 dp-8) until it is recorded, then that it waits on a VA POC, is confirmed, or lapsed. The VA POC's Exemption queue answers `confirmations` beside `requests`: the recorded VAs waiting on a confirmation (recorded, not confirmed, still Exempted), the earliest recorded first, with `cycle`, `quarter`, the dates, the evidence and `sodConflict` (the confirmation's own rule, `Submissions.conflictedAt`). They are read from every Cycle the app runs, not only the one on screen, because a Q4 one waits after its Cycle has closed and the shell's Cycle choice falls back from a closed Cycle (ADR 0044 item 8). One `EXEMPTIONS_VIEWED` entry counts both lists. The tab shows Confirm VA (step-up) per row, or "the other VA POC confirms".

9. **Endorsed Late after a lapse is judged against the stored `endorse_due`** (decided 2026-10-26, docs/agents/reviewer-decisions.md). Item 5 moves a lapsed VA Submission's `endorse_due` to the later of the Quarter's last day and the Cycle's close, and the endorsement keeps judging Endorsed Late against the stored date (ADR 0042), so an endorsement after the Quarter but before the close is not flagged late. Rather than a second rule for the lapse's own deadline, the management summary shows why: an Exemption Lapsed count per Quarter and a column per System, in the workbook and the PDF alike (ADR 0045 item 3, ADR 0054 item 3), read from the same lasting flag as the dashboards (item 7). Judging a flag against a window a later rule may move is done against the stored window, with the move shown.

Migration numbering: V37 sorts after V33 (ticket 40) and V34 (ticket 43), whose notification CHECKs it rebuilds with the union of every value added before it, and after V36 (ticket 49), merged before it. The new columns refer only to `app_user`, so the disposal order (ADR 0050) needs no change: an `exemption` row goes with its VA Submission as before.

Not done here, on purpose:
- A lapsed SMO/Tech Lead role coming back when the lapse hands the work back (spec *Release 1 decisions*, ac-3): no role-return mechanism exists yet; the role-lapse ticket owns it.
- An SMO inbox row for a Q4 conditional Exemption after its Cycle closes: the shell falls back from a closed Cycle, so the SMO/Tech Lead reaches the VA Submission through the approval notification's link.
- Any Playwright scenario (ticket 53).

Consequences:
- `Submissions.Gate` has a flag for the closed-Cycle check; a new gate refuses a closed Cycle unless it is built with `afterClose`.
- The Exemption queue lists two kinds of work; its `cycle` names the Cycle of the `requests` only.
- `Submissions.Submission`, `Submissions.Summary` and every dashboard row carry one more field.

Amends ADR 0044 item 6 (the exception is a gate kind and a job) and ADR 0048 items 5 and 7 (the conditional Exemption's resolution, the queue's second list).
