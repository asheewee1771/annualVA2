# The Cyber Manager's inbox and Quarter board read the review scope, and every state is named

Status: accepted

Ticket 27 (stories 48 and 49) gives the Cyber Manager two tabs: an action inbox of the VA Submissions of the Systems assigned to them, Overdue first, and a Quarter board with one column per state. Both are read models in the `dashboard` module, like the SMO inbox (ADR 0019): they store nothing and change nothing. Six decisions follow.

1. **One dashboard reader, by need.** `DashboardSystems.in(cycle, need)` lists the caller's active Systems for a need (`Need.SUBMIT` for the SMO inbox, `Need.REVIEW` for the Cyber Manager's inbox and board, through `AccessGuard.scope`, ADR 0013), with each System's VA Submission in the Cycle (or Not Started), the day its current window ends, the Overdue flag the sweep stored (ADR 0033) and, apart, the Systems in Quarters Handled outside the app (`HandledOutside`). The board reads one Quarter only. Every dashboard reads it, so none can disagree with another about whose Systems they show or when each is due; the Cyber Manager's two add `ReviewMarkers` (item 3 and 4) over the same rows, and the VA POC's board (ticket 33) is the same reader for `Need.ENDORSE`. The headings are one `InboxGroup`, and `SystemState.isFinal()` says once which states are final. Both endpoints are `hasRole('CYBER_MANAGER')`: an SMO/Tech Lead, a VA POC, an Auditor or a person with no role gets 403, no session 401. The VA POC's board (ticket 33) opens the board to `VA_POC` with the endorse scope.
2. **The inbox order is the server's.** `GET /api/dashboards/cm-inbox?cycle=` lists every System assigned to the caller, Not Started included, Overdue first, then by `dueOn` (the last day of the window its state waits on), then by Quarter and System ID; a final or frozen state, which waits on no window, has `dueOn` null and comes last. `dueOn` comes from the one window rule, `current_window_due` (V20, ADR 0033 item 2), read with the VA Submission, and for a Not Started System asked of the same rule with its Quarter's dates (`Submissions.notStartedDueOn`). The SMO inbox's `nextActionDueOn` is the same day, so a Returned for Rework VA Submission is due on `review_due` in both inboxes; ticket 31 (the guided rework queue) may revisit that date, in the rule and nowhere else. Each item carries `group`: In Review is `NEEDS_YOUR_ACTION`, Endorsed, Exempted, Withdrawn and Not Endorsed are `DONE`, and anything else is `WAITING_ON_OTHERS`. `cycle` behaves as in the SMO inbox (ADR 0019 item 3).
3. **Counts, not content.** Each item, and each card on the board, carries `confirmed` and `awaitingReview`, the Findings of its VA Submission whose current proposal the Cyber Manager confirmed and those still PENDING, named as the readiness counts are (reviewer decisions, "Readiness counts name awaiting review and unproposed explicitly"). `Findings.reviewCounts` answers only VA Submissions of Systems the caller may see. Counts carry no report content, so neither read is audited (ADR 0018, ADR 0019); the architecture test now lets `dashboard` read `finding`.
4. **The segregation-of-duties marker reads the participation record.** `sodConflict` is true when the caller acted on that VA Submission at a step other than reviewing (`Submissions.actedAtAnotherStep`, over `submission_participation`), for example because they started or submitted it as its SMO/Tech Lead before a Register change made them its Cyber Manager. It is a marker only: the review engine (ticket 28) refuses the review itself with `sod-conflict`. `SubmissionStep` gains `REVIEW`, which ticket 28 records.
5. **The board has a column for every state, in the spec's order.** `GET /api/dashboards/quarter-board?cycle=&quarter=` answers `cycle`, `quarter` and ten `columns` in the order of the spec's state table (Not Started, Draft, In Review, Returned for Rework, Awaiting Endorsement, Endorsed, Exemption Requested, Exempted, Withdrawn, Not Endorsed), each present even when empty, so no System falls off the board whatever state it is in. `quarter` is 1 to 4 (anything else 400); omitted, it is the Quarter the Cycle shows today (`VaCycle.quarterShownOn`): the one today falls in, even one Handled outside the app (which then shows its Systems apart, below), before the Cycle starts its first live Quarter and after the Cycle's last day its Q4. In a Quarter Handled outside the app every column is empty and the Systems are listed under `handledOutsideApp`.
6. **Every state is named in `SubmissionState`.** The table always accepted the nine states; the enum named only the three a command entered, so a VA Submission in any other state would have failed every read. It now names all nine, so the dashboards and the VA Submission read show a VA Submission whatever state it is in; the commands that enter the later states still arrive with their tickets. The SMO inbox follows: Awaiting Endorsement waits on the VA POCs (`AWAIT_ENDORSEMENT`, due the Quarter's last day), Exemption Requested on their decision (`AWAIT_EXEMPTION_DECISION`, no due date), and a final state is `DONE` with `nextAction` and `nextActionDueOn` null. The dashboards share one `SystemState` (Not Started plus the nine).

Consequences: the review scope, the Overdue flag and the window rule each have one reader, so the inbox, the board and the sweep agree; neither read is paged (a Cyber Manager reviews a handful of Systems a Quarter); a Withdrawn VA Submission belongs to a retired System, which the Register scope no longer lists, so the Withdrawn column stays empty for a Cyber Manager until the Register's effects (ticket 43) say otherwise.

Amends ADR 0019 (the SMO inbox names every state) and ADR 0033 (the Cyber Manager inbox's Overdue-first order is built).

Amended by ADR 0035: item 4's marker no longer reads participation only. `sodConflict` is true when segregation of duties refuses the caller's review by the one rule the review itself is refused by: they acted on the VA Submission at another step, or are assigned to another step of its System (its SMO/Tech Lead, say). `Submissions.actedAtAnotherStep` is now `Submissions.conflictedAt`, and ticket 28 records the `REVIEW` step.

Amended by ADR 0036: the Quarter board is open to `VA_POC`, read for `Need.ENDORSE` (every System), and a VA POC's cards mark no segregation-of-duties conflict until the endorsing step is recorded.
