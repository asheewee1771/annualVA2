# Records are disposed of from a list a VA POC approves, through one audited path in the append-only tables

Status: accepted (items 2, 3, 4 and 8 provisional, see below)

Ticket 49 (story 87; spec "Retention", "Disposal", ac-2) keeps records for the VA Cycle plus 3 years, a superseded Scan Report file's content until its VA Submission is Endorsed and an export for 24 hours, then hard-deletes them from a list a VA POC approves with a fresh sign-in, and logs it. It adds one module, `retention`, on top beside `export`, one migration (V149), and one screen.

1. **The list.** The retention job (`DisposalPlanner.plan()`, daily at 02:30 Singapore time; `RetentionJob`) writes what is due as a `disposal_list` in `DRAFT` with one `disposal_item` per record or group: `SUBMISSION` (a VA Submission and everything that hangs off it), `FILE` (each of its Scan Report files, content and row), `CYCLE` (the Cycle's records about no one VA Submission), `SYSTEM` (a retired System), `SUPERSEDED_FILE` (a superseded file's content only), `EXPORT` (an export's row) and `AUDIT_RANGE` (the audit entries before the list's cutoff, with their count), each with a reason code (`CYCLE_RETENTION_ENDED`, `SUPERSEDED_AFTER_ENDORSEMENT`, `EXPORT_EXPIRED`, `SYSTEM_RETIRED`). Items are ids and codes only, with no foreign key to their targets, so the list stays as the record of what was disposed of. A pass that finds exactly what the waiting DRAFT holds (a SHA-256 fingerprint of the cutoff and the items) keeps it, so the list a VA POC is reading does not change under them; anything else supersedes it (`SUPERSEDED`) with a new DRAFT. At most one list is `DRAFT` or `APPROVED` (a unique partial index), and nothing is listed while one is `APPROVED`. `GET /api/disposal-lists` (VA POC) gives the newest 20 lists with their items, each System named through `RegisterSystems.list()`.

2. **When a Cycle's retention ends.** *Provisional:* a Cycle's records are kept through the third anniversary of the business date it closed on (`va_cycle.closed_at`, ADR 0044) and are due the day after. A Cycle that has not closed is never due, so unfinished work is never listed. The alternatives were Q4's last day plus 3 years (earlier, but a Cycle stays open four weeks after Q4 for late endorsements) and the end of the calendar year of the close plus 3 years (later, and an extra rule).

3. **The cutoff.** *Provisional:* the records of the due Cycles that carry no Cycle (the audit log, the Register previews, notifications about no VA Submission) are those written before the first day of the Cycle after the newest due one (`CycleCalendar.startOf`). Nothing of a later Cycle is disposed of early; the four weeks a Cycle stays open after its Q4 overlap the next Cycle and wait for its disposal. The alternative, the due Cycle's close, would dispose of up to four weeks of the next Cycle's audit entries a year early.

4. **Retired Systems and exports.** *Provisional:* a System retired before the cutoff whose every VA Submission and kept Register row is in a due Cycle is listed (`RegisterSystems.retiredBefore`, guard-less for the job alone, which an architecture test names); its `not_started_overdue`, `cycle_register_system`, notifications and role grants go before its row. An export's row is listed once the export job has marked it `EXPIRED` (its file was removed at 24 hours, ADR 0045), or a day after it `FAILED`.

5. **Approval.** `POST /api/disposal-lists/{id}/approve` (role `VA_POC`) checks, in order: step-up (`StepUp.require("disposal-approve")`, spec ac-2), the list (`404`), and that it is the DRAFT waiting (`409 list-not-draft` for one superseded, approved or executed, audited `REFUSED`). It marks the list `APPROVED` with who and when and writes `DISPOSAL_APPROVED`. One VA POC approves; the list is the job's, not a person's, so there is no segregation of duties to apply.

6. **The executor.** `DisposalExecutor.execute()` (every 5 minutes, and before each listing) deletes the file-store objects the approved list's records hold first, outside any transaction (the clean copies and any quarantined bytes of the listed VA Submissions' files, the superseded files' clean copies, any export file still held). A store that cannot delete stops the pass with nothing changed in the database, and the next pass tries again (deleting what is gone is not an error), so no record is ever gone while its content is still held. Then, in one transaction holding the retention advisory lock, `DisposalStore.dispose` deletes in foreign-key order, reading every target from the list's own items: the mail outbox and notifications; the return items and returns; the Finding reviews, batch reviews and justification reviews; it clears `finding.current_proposal_id` and the review state, then deletes the proposals, the batches, the occurrences and the Findings; the file stages and every file of the VA Submissions in one statement (a rescan and the file it replaced reference each other, checked at the end of the statement); the Scan Report slots, Declarations and participation; the VA Submissions; the Cycles' and retired Systems' `not_started_overdue` (it names `va_system`, ticket 26) and kept Register rows, the Register previews before the cutoff and the retired Systems' role grants; the Systems; the exports; the audit entries before the cutoff. It stamps the superseded files' `content_disposed_at`, marks the list `EXECUTED`, and writes one tombstone, `DISPOSAL_EXECUTED`, with `disposal_list_id` and the counts `submissions`, `files`, `superseded_files`, `exports`, `systems` and `audit_entries` (V149 adds these keys to the allow-list). `va_cycle` and `declaration_text` are kept: the lifecycle job would reopen a Cycle with no row, and the wordings belong to no Cycle.

7. **One path through the append-only tables.** V149 replaces the functions behind the append-only triggers of `audit_entry` (V5), `finding_status_proposal` (V14), `bulk_batch` (V15) and the tables that share `declaration_append_only` (V19, V21, V129, V130). An `UPDATE` or `TRUNCATE` is refused always, as before. A `DELETE` is allowed only when the transaction-local setting `annualva.disposal_list` names a list that is `APPROVED` (`disposal_running()`), which only the executor sets, inside the transaction that then marks the list `EXECUTED`; a setting naming a DRAFT, an unknown or a malformed list opens nothing, and `declaration_text` is never opened. `audit_entry` also has a per-row trigger that refuses any entry not before the running list's cutoff, so even the executor cannot remove the tombstone or anything after the cutoff. Ordinary writes see the triggers exactly as before (Seam 1 tests on the application's own connection prove both). As ADR 0014 says, the triggers guard against mistakes and application bugs, not against the owner of the database login.

8. **Superseded files early, and every other removal.** As the reviewers decided (2026-10-18), a superseded file of an Endorsed VA Submission is disposed of early by deleting only its content: its `report_file` row, name, hash and links stay until its Cycle is disposed of, because a RESCAN proposal and the rescan reference it. `report_file.content_disposed_at` records it (a CHECK allows it on a superseded file only); the file view gains `contentDisposedAt`, the SMO's Scan Report card says "Content disposed of" and offers no download, and a download answers `410 disposed`, audited `FILE_DOWNLOADED` `REFUSED` with `reason` `disposed`. *Provisional:* only Endorsed counts, as the spec says; an Exempted or Not Endorsed VA Submission keeps its superseded files until its Cycle is disposed of. Nothing else in the application hard-deletes a record before its retention ends: such a removal is a status or a flag (a soft delete), and the append-only tables refuse it.

9. **The screen.** `/va-poc/disposal`, a tab of the VA POC area: the list waiting, its counts by kind, every item with the System, Cycle and reason, and Approve (step-up; the app explains a `403 step-up-required` and brings the VA POC back to press Approve again); then the lists disposed of before.

Consequences:
- `retention` reads `access`, `audit`, `boundary`, `cycle`, `register` and `web`; nothing reads it (architecture test). Its one store, `DisposalStore`, deletes from other modules' tables: the deletion order is one rule, written once, beside the triggers it relies on.
- A new table that references a VA Submission, a Finding, a file, a System or a notification must be added to `DisposalStore.dispose` in its foreign-key place, or the disposal fails on the foreign key and nothing is deleted (the transaction rolls back and the list stays `APPROVED`).
- The test reset deletes the disposal lists, and `GET /api/test/rows` reports how many rows each disposed table holds.
- Amends ADR 0014 (the audit log's trigger now lets an approved disposal through), ADR 0027 and ADR 0028 (the proposals' and batches' triggers likewise) and ADR 0029 item 7 (a superseded file's content is disposed of once Endorsed).
