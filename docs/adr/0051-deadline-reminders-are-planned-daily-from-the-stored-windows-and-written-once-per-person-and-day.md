# Deadline reminders are planned daily from the stored windows and written once per person and day

Status: accepted (items 3 and 4 provisional, awaiting a human decision)

Ticket 38 (stories 88, 89 and 90) reminds the SMO/Tech Lead, the Cyber Manager and the VA POCs of their deadlines by email and in the app. It adds one migration (`V149_38`), a read of waiting work in `submission`, a planner and its daily job in `notification`, ten `NoticeAction`s and four mail templates, and no endpoint.

1. **A daily planner over the stored state.** `DeadlineReminders.plan` (in `notification`, which owns the reminder schedule) reads the clock once, takes today's business date in `Asia/Singapore` and asks `WaitingWork.on(today)` (in `submission`, which owns windows and Overdue) for every piece of work that waits on a window: each VA Submission whose state has a `current_window_due` (V20), and each System with none in the Cycle (Not Started, due by `NOT_STARTED`'s window for its Quarter), each with its due date by that one rule and its Overdue flag as the sweep stored it (ADR 0033: the flag is read, never computed). `WaitingWork` looks where the sweep looks: the active Systems of every VA Cycle that has not closed (`RegisterSystems.everyActive(year)`, which skips the access guard: it acts for nobody, and `ArchitectureTest` now names it beside the sweep and the lifecycle), in Quarters not Handled outside the app. So a retired System (whose VA Submission is Withdrawn), the final states and the frozen Exemption Requested, which wait on no window, are never reminded. `DeadlineReminderJob` runs it at 08:00 Singapore time (`annualva.reminders.cron`) when scheduling is on; a Seam 1 test moves the clock and calls `POST /api/test/jobs/reminders`.

2. **The schedule, written once.** The person whose step it is, by the state, as the Overdue notice of ADR 0043 item 3 addresses them:
   - SMO/Tech Lead (Not Started, Draft, Returned for Rework) and Cyber Manager (In Review): 5 days before the due date (`SUBMIT_DUE_SOON`, `REVIEW_DUE_SOON`), on it (`…_DUE_TODAY`), and every 7 days after the day the sweep's Overdue message went (the day after the due date: due + 8, + 15, …) while the flag is set (`…_STILL_OVERDUE`). The sweep's own Overdue message is the first of the weekly series, so the planner never repeats it. Returned for Rework waits on the Review Window (ADR 0033 item 2), so its SMO/Tech Lead is reminded against the end of week 4, not week 2.
   - Both VA POCs (Awaiting Endorsement): 14 and 7 days before the Quarter's last day (`ENDORSE_DUE_IN_TWO_WEEKS`, `ENDORSE_DUE_IN_ONE_WEEK`), sent as a pair awaiting `ENDORSEMENT`, so either VA POC's endorsement or return clears both (story 69).
   - A reminder is due on its exact day. A morning the application is down sends nothing and is not caught up: a late "due in 5 days" would say the wrong thing, and the next reminder in the series still comes.

3. **Copies go on the weekly Overdue reminders only** (provisional). Story 88 reads "then weekly while Overdue with the Cyber Manager copied in", and the implementation plan attaches the copy to the weekly message; story 89 makes the Cyber Manager "the one copied in on that System's SMO reminders". The planner sends the copy with each weekly Overdue reminder: the Cyber Manager for the SMO/Tech Lead's (`SUBMIT_OVERDUE_COPY`, linking their inbox, since a Draft is not theirs to open), each VA POC for the Cyber Manager's (`REVIEW_OVERDUE_COPY`, linking the VA Submission, an item of their own that nothing clears: `Notifications.toEachVaPoc`). The relay has no cc, so each copy is a notification and a mail of its own. Copying the 5-day and due-day reminders too is a two-line change in `DeadlineReminders.remind`.

4. **Mail templates** (provisional, MCNS onboarding in ticket 57). Every reminder is a required message (spec, *Notifications*) and is mailed: `REMINDER_DUE_SOON` (the 5-day, 2-week and 1-week reminders), `REMINDER_DUE` (the last day), `OVERDUE_WEEKLY` and `REMINDER_COPY`, each with its own `annualva.mail.templates` id (`ANNUALVA_MAIL_TEMPLATE_*`). The `action` value carries the app's sentence, so one template serves the SMO/Tech Lead, the Cyber Manager and the VA POCs; ticket 57's list names separate Cyber Manager and VA POC copy variants, which would be one more constant and id each. The values are ADR 0046's allow-list, nothing more.

5. **Once per person, key and day, safe to rerun.** `Notice` gains an optional `once` key; a reminder's is its action, System, due date and day (`Notice.remindingOn`), checked by a CHECK to that shape. `notification` gains `dedupe_key` with `UNIQUE (recipient_id, dedupe_key)`, and every insert is `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`; a notice of a move has no key (NULLs never conflict) and is unchanged. The mail is queued only when the row was written, so a rerun of the same day, or a second instance's run at the same time (which waits on the first's uncommitted row and then inserts nothing), writes and mails nothing more. This is the idempotency of ADR 0012 without a `scheduler_lock` row. Each reminder is written in a transaction of its own; one that cannot be written is logged (`reminder-failed`, with the System's id and the exception type, never an address or a value) and the rest still go out; a rerun the same day writes what is missing (proven by a Seam 1 test that makes every notification write fail and then reruns).

6. **A System added after its window** (reviewer decisions, 2026-10-23). Until Release 2 gives an added System its own windows, the sweep flags a System added after its Quarter's Submission Window at once, so its SMO/Tech Lead gets the Overdue message straight away and then the weekly reminders by its Quarter's dates, with the copy to its Cyber Manager. `va_system.added_on` is not used. Seam 1 proves it.

Not done here, on purpose: a weekly Overdue reminder to the VA POCs for an endorsement past the Quarter's last day (story 90 asks only for the two before it; the sweep's `ENDORSE_OVERDUE` tells them once); reminders for Unlinked or Unreachable SMOs beyond what is sent to everyone (ticket 39); the weekly digest of FYIs (story 93, Release 2).

Consequences:
- A new reminder is a `NoticeAction`, its `MailTemplate.of` mapping, and a migration widening `notification_action_check` that sorts after `V149_38` and keeps the union.
- Every deployed profile needs the four new template ids; `sit` has placeholders until ticket 57.
- The reminders read what the sweep stored, so a weekly reminder waits for the sweep to flag the work (it runs hourly, so on the reminder's day it has).

Amends ADR 0033 (the Overdue flag now drives the weekly reminder), ADR 0043 (the `Notifications` port writes a keyed notice at most once per person, and `toEachVaPoc` sends an item to each VA POC that no decision clears) and ADR 0046 item 2 (the reminders' four templates).
